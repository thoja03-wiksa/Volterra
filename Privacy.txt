<h1>Voxel Survivors – Privacy Policy</h1>

<p>Last updated: 5 November 2026</p>

<p>
Voxel Survivors ("the app") is a mobile game developed and published by
iHoja Studios ("we", "us" or "our").
This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the app.
</p>


<h2>Data we collect ourselves</h2>

<p>
We do not currently require users to create an account and we do not directly
collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number
or physical address.
</p>

<p>
Game progress, settings, resources, unlocked content and other gameplay data
are currently stored locally on your device.
This information is not uploaded to our servers.
</p>

<p>
Local game data may be removed if you uninstall the app, clear the app's data
or reset your device.
</p>


<h2>Advertising – Google AdMob</h2>

<p>
Voxel Survivors may display advertisements, including optional rewarded video
ads, using Google AdMob.
</p>

<p>
When advertisements are displayed, Google and its advertising partners may
automatically collect or process certain information, including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Advertising identifiers</li>
  <li>IP address</li>
  <li>Approximate location derived from network information</li>
  <li>Device information</li>
  <li>Operating system and app information</li>
  <li>Advertising interactions and impressions</li>
  <li>Diagnostic and performance information</li>
</ul>

<p>
This information may be used to deliver advertisements, prevent fraud,
measure advertising performance and, where permitted and consented to,
provide personalised advertising.
</p>

<p>
We do not directly receive or store this advertising information in our own
user database. It is processed by Google and its advertising technology
partners according to their respective privacy policies.
</p>


<h2>Advertising consent</h2>

<p>
For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and
Switzerland, the app may display a consent message before advertising data
is used where consent is legally required.
</p>

<p>
Depending on your choices and applicable law, advertisements may be
personalised or non-personalised.
</p>

<p>
You may also manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your
Android device settings.
</p>

<p>
For more information about how Google processes data, please see:
</p>

<p>
<a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">
How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
</a>
</p>

<p>
<a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">
Google Privacy Policy
</a>
</p>


<h2>In-app purchases</h2>

<p>
If Voxel Survivors offers digital purchases in the future, payments may be
processed through Google Play.
</p>

<p>
We do not receive or store your full payment card or banking information.
Payment information is processed by Google according to Google Play's terms
and privacy policies.
</p>


<h2>Children</h2>

<p>
Voxel Survivors is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
</p>

<p>
Advertising services used by the app may apply additional protections when
required by applicable law and Google policies.
</p>


<h2>Data retention and deletion</h2>

<p>
Because gameplay progress is currently stored locally on your device, we do
not maintain a server-side copy of that gameplay information.
</p>

<p>
You can remove locally stored gameplay data by clearing the app's storage or
uninstalling the app.
</p>

<p>
Data processed independently by third-party services such as Google may be
retained according to their own privacy and retention policies.
</p>


<h2>Data security</h2>

<p>
We take reasonable steps to minimise the amount of personal information
processed by the app.
</p>

<p>
Third-party services used by the app are responsible for protecting
information processed through their systems according to their respective
security and privacy practices.
</p>


<h2>Your privacy rights</h2>

<p>
Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, you may have rights
relating to personal data such as the right to access, correct, delete or
restrict certain processing and the right to withdraw consent where consent
is the legal basis for processing.
</p>

<p>
Because we do not currently maintain user accounts or directly store personal
user information on our servers, requests relating specifically to advertising
data may need to be directed to Google.
</p>

<p>
You may still contact us if you have a privacy-related question and we will
assist where reasonably possible.
</p>


<h2>Changes to this Privacy Policy</h2>

<p>
We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, for example if we add
accounts, cloud saves, analytics, additional advertising services or other
online features.
</p>

<p>
The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page and
the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
</p>

VOLTERRA: Machine Siege – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

VOLTERRA: Machine Siege ("the app", Android package com.volterra.game) is a mobile game developed and published by iHoja Studios ("we", "us" or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes and how.

Summary

  • The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your game progress and your optional player account are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive them.
  • Voice commands use the microphone only while you use the voice-command button. Speech may be turned into text by Google's speech service (see below). We do not record or store audio.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, resources, unlocked content and other gameplay data are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

Optional player account. You can create a player account in the app with an email address, a password and a player name. In the current version this account exists only on your device: the password and the one-time recovery code are stored only as secure hashes, and nothing is sent to us or to any server. The account separates the progress of different players on the same device.

Local data is removed if you delete your account in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Microphone and voice commands

The app asks for microphone permission only when you first use a voice command. The microphone is used only while you hold or tap the voice-command button, to turn your spoken order into text.

  • If your phone has an offline speech pack for the chosen language, recognition happens on the device.
  • Otherwise, if the setting Online recognition is ON (default) and you are online, your spoken command is sent to Google's speech recognition service by the Android system to be turned into text. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can turn Online recognition OFF in Settings. Recognition then happens on the device only.
  • We do not record, store or receive audio or the recognised text.

In-app purchases

If the app offers digital purchases, payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card or bank details. Google processes payment information under Google Play's terms and privacy policies.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not show advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Account → Delete account, enter your password and type DELETE to confirm. The account and its saved progress are removed from the device immediately.

Without the app: because the account is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage (Android Settings → Apps → VOLTERRA → Storage → Clear storage) deletes the account and all its data. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

If you have any question about deleting your data, contact us at the email address below and we will reply within 30 days.

Data security

We keep the data the app handles to a minimum. Passwords and recovery codes are never stored in plain text.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU GDPR, you have rights relating to personal data, such as the right to access, correct or delete it and to withdraw consent. Because we do not currently store your personal data on our own servers, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app (see Deleting your account and data). For voice processed by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy. You can always contact us with privacy questions.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We will update this policy before the app adds online features such as online accounts, friends, online multiplayer, cloud saves or AI-assisted voice commands. The latest version is always published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
